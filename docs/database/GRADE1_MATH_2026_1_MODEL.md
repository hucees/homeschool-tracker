# Grade 1 Mathematics — Curriculum Model 2026.1

This document mirrors Migration 004 and is the human-readable specification for the first curriculum course loaded into Homeschool Tracker.

## Course rules

- 36 instructional weeks, four 9-week quarters.
- Five 30-minute mathematics lessons per week (180 daily lessons).
- Standard daily cycle: instruction → guided practice → independent practice → application → assessment.
- Quarter mastery checks occur in Weeks 9, 18, 27, and 36.
- Competency mastery requires at least two independent demonstrations; the direct assessment threshold is stored per competency.
- The 2026.1 release remains `draft` until the broader curriculum is ready to be published and permanently locked.

## Units

- **1-MATH-U01 — Numbers and Counting** (Q1): Build number sense through 120 and recognize counting patterns.
- **1-MATH-U02 — Place Value and Comparison** (Q1): Understand tens and ones and compare two-digit numbers.
- **1-MATH-U03 — Addition and Subtraction Within 20** (Q2): Develop strategies, fluency, operation relationships, and one-step problem solving.
- **1-MATH-U04 — Equations and Unknowns** (Q3): Understand equality and find unknown values in addition and subtraction equations.
- **1-MATH-U05 — Measurement and Time** (Q3): Measure and compare length and tell time to the hour and half-hour.
- **1-MATH-U06 — Data** (Q4): Organize, represent, and interpret simple data sets.
- **1-MATH-U07 — Geometry and Equal Shares** (Q4): Reason about 2D/3D shapes and partition shapes into equal shares.
- **1-MATH-U08 — Place-Value Operations Within 100** (Q4): Use tens-and-ones reasoning to add within 100, find 10 more/less, and subtract multiples of 10.

## Mastery specifications

### 1-MATH-01 — Count, read, write, and represent numbers to 120

**Mastery objective:** Independently count, read, write, order, and represent whole numbers from 0 through 120 using numerals, objects, base-ten representations, or number lines with at least 85% accuracy across two separate demonstrations.

**Required evidence:** A written/visual number-representation check plus an oral or hands-on counting task that includes numbers above 100.

**Prerequisites:** K-MATH-01, K-MATH-02, K-MATH-03, K-MATH-04

**Primary lesson plan:** 5 lessons — 1 instruction, 1 guided practice, 1 independent practice, 1 application, 1 assessment.

### 1-MATH-02 — Count forward and backward from different starting numbers

**Mastery objective:** Given teacher-selected starting numbers within 0–120, count forward and backward by ones across a stated range without needing to restart at 0 or 1, with at least 85% accuracy across two separate demonstrations.

**Required evidence:** An oral counting demonstration from multiple starting points plus a written missing-number or number-line task.

**Prerequisites:** 1-MATH-01, K-MATH-06

**Primary lesson plan:** 5 lessons — 1 instruction, 1 guided practice, 1 independent practice, 1 application, 1 assessment.

### 1-MATH-03 — Recognize and use counting patterns by 2s, 5s, and 10s

**Mastery objective:** Identify, continue, and generate counting patterns by 2s, 5s, and 10s and explain the repeating pattern in the ones digits with at least 85% accuracy across two separate demonstrations.

**Required evidence:** A skip-counting performance task plus a written pattern-completion check using 2s, 5s, and 10s.

**Prerequisites:** 1-MATH-01, 1-MATH-02

**Primary lesson plan:** 5 lessons — 1 instruction, 1 guided practice, 1 independent practice, 1 application, 1 assessment.

### 1-MATH-04 — Understand tens and ones

**Mastery objective:** Represent any two-digit number as groups of tens and leftover ones, state the value of each digit, and compose/decompose the number in at least two forms with at least 85% accuracy across two separate demonstrations.

**Required evidence:** A base-ten model or drawing task plus a written place-value check requiring composition and decomposition of two-digit numbers.

**Prerequisites:** 1-MATH-01, K-MATH-07

**Primary lesson plan:** 10 lessons — 2 instruction, 2 guided practice, 2 independent practice, 2 application, 2 assessment.

### 1-MATH-05 — Compare two-digit numbers using >, <, and =

**Mastery objective:** Compare pairs of two-digit numbers by reasoning about tens first and ones second and correctly record the relationship using >, <, or = with at least 85% accuracy across two separate demonstrations.

**Required evidence:** A written comparison check plus an oral explanation using place-value reasoning for at least three comparison problems.

**Prerequisites:** 1-MATH-04

**Primary lesson plan:** 10 lessons — 2 instruction, 2 guided practice, 2 independent practice, 2 application, 2 assessment.

### 1-MATH-06 — Add within 20

**Mastery objective:** Solve addition problems with sums through 20 using drawings, objects, number lines, making-ten, doubles, or related strategies and explain at least one strategy, with at least 85% accuracy across two separate demonstrations.

**Required evidence:** A mixed written addition check within 20 plus a strategy demonstration or student explanation using a model.

**Prerequisites:** 1-MATH-01, K-MATH-08

**Primary lesson plan:** 10 lessons — 2 instruction, 2 guided practice, 2 independent practice, 2 application, 2 assessment.

### 1-MATH-07 — Subtract within 20

**Mastery objective:** Solve subtraction problems within 20 using drawings, objects, number lines, decomposing, counting on, or related strategies and explain at least one strategy, with at least 85% accuracy across two separate demonstrations.

**Required evidence:** A mixed written subtraction check within 20 plus a strategy demonstration or student explanation using a model.

**Prerequisites:** 1-MATH-01, K-MATH-09

**Primary lesson plan:** 10 lessons — 2 instruction, 2 guided practice, 2 independent practice, 2 application, 2 assessment.

### 1-MATH-08 — Develop addition and subtraction fact fluency within 10

**Mastery objective:** Accurately and efficiently recall or derive mixed addition and subtraction facts within 10 with at least 90% accuracy across two separate fluency checks without relying on counting every item from one.

**Required evidence:** Two mixed fact-fluency checks on different days, supported by teacher observation that the student uses efficient strategies rather than recounting every fact.

**Prerequisites:** 1-MATH-06, 1-MATH-07

**Primary lesson plan:** 5 lessons — 1 instruction, 1 guided practice, 1 independent practice, 1 application, 1 assessment.

### 1-MATH-09 — Use the relationship between addition and subtraction

**Mastery objective:** Use inverse relationships and fact families to connect addition and subtraction, generate related equations, and use one operation to check the other with at least 85% accuracy across two separate demonstrations.

**Required evidence:** A fact-family/equation check plus an oral or written explanation showing how an addition fact can solve or verify a related subtraction fact.

**Prerequisites:** 1-MATH-06, 1-MATH-07

**Primary lesson plan:** 5 lessons — 1 instruction, 1 guided practice, 1 independent practice, 1 application, 1 assessment.

### 1-MATH-10 — Solve one-step addition and subtraction word problems

**Mastery objective:** Represent and solve one-step join, separate, part-part-whole, and compare situations within 20 using an equation, drawing, object model, or number line and identify the unknown, with at least 85% accuracy across two separate demonstrations.

**Required evidence:** A mixed one-step word-problem assessment plus one independent model-and-explain task in which the student chooses the operation and justifies it.

**Prerequisites:** 1-MATH-06, 1-MATH-07, 1-MATH-09

**Primary lesson plan:** 10 lessons — 2 instruction, 2 guided practice, 2 independent practice, 2 application, 2 assessment.

### 1-MATH-11 — Understand equations and determine unknowns

**Mastery objective:** Determine an unknown whole number in an addition or subtraction equation within 20, recognize whether simple equations are true or false, and explain the meaning of the equals sign, with at least 85% accuracy across two separate demonstrations.

**Required evidence:** A written unknown-equation check plus an oral or hands-on balance/equality explanation using equations in more than one unknown position.

**Prerequisites:** 1-MATH-06, 1-MATH-07, 1-MATH-09

**Primary lesson plan:** 10 lessons — 2 instruction, 2 guided practice, 2 independent practice, 2 application, 2 assessment.

### 1-MATH-12 — Measure length using repeated equal-size units

**Mastery objective:** Measure the length of an object by laying equal-size units end-to-end with no gaps or overlaps, report the measurement with its unit, and explain why equal units are required, with at least 85% accuracy across two separate demonstrations.

**Required evidence:** A hands-on measurement task using repeated units plus a recorded measurement sheet or teacher check of several objects.

**Prerequisites:** 1-MATH-01, K-MATH-13

**Primary lesson plan:** 10 lessons — 2 instruction, 2 guided practice, 2 independent practice, 2 application, 2 assessment.

### 1-MATH-13 — Compare and order lengths

**Mastery objective:** Directly or indirectly compare and order the lengths of at least three objects using longer than, shorter than, and equal/same length language with at least 85% accuracy across two separate demonstrations.

**Required evidence:** A hands-on ordering task plus a written or oral comparison check using direct and indirect comparisons.

**Prerequisites:** 1-MATH-12

**Primary lesson plan:** 5 lessons — 1 instruction, 1 guided practice, 1 independent practice, 1 application, 1 assessment.

### 1-MATH-14 — Tell and write time to the hour and half-hour

**Mastery objective:** Read, model, and write times to the hour and half-hour on analog and digital clocks and connect the hour and minute hands to the stated time with at least 85% accuracy across two separate demonstrations.

**Required evidence:** An analog/digital clock matching assessment plus a hands-on clock-setting task covering both hour and half-hour times.

**Prerequisites:** 1-MATH-01

**Primary lesson plan:** 15 lessons — 3 instruction, 3 guided practice, 3 independent practice, 3 application, 3 assessment.

### 1-MATH-15 — Organize and interpret data

**Mastery objective:** Sort data into categories, count the number in each category, and answer comparison questions about how many more, how many fewer, or how many total, with at least 85% accuracy across two separate demonstrations.

**Required evidence:** A category-sorting/data table task plus an interpretation check requiring totals and comparison questions.

**Prerequisites:** 1-MATH-01, K-MATH-14

**Primary lesson plan:** 5 lessons — 1 instruction, 1 guided practice, 1 independent practice, 1 application, 1 assessment.

### 1-MATH-16 — Represent data with pictures, charts, and simple graphs

**Mastery objective:** Create or complete a picture representation, chart, or simple graph from a small data set and accurately use the representation to answer questions with at least 85% accuracy across two separate demonstrations.

**Required evidence:** A student-created data display plus a short written or oral interpretation check based on that display.

**Prerequisites:** 1-MATH-15

**Primary lesson plan:** 5 lessons — 1 instruction, 1 guided practice, 1 independent practice, 1 application, 1 assessment.

### 1-MATH-17 — Reason with, draw, build, and compose 2D and 3D shapes

**Mastery objective:** Identify defining attributes of common 2D and 3D shapes, draw or build examples, and compose larger shapes from smaller shapes while explaining the attributes used, with at least 85% accuracy across two separate demonstrations.

**Required evidence:** A shape identification/attribute check plus a hands-on drawing, building, or composing performance task.

**Prerequisites:** K-MATH-11

**Primary lesson plan:** 5 lessons — 1 instruction, 1 guided practice, 1 independent practice, 1 application, 1 assessment.

### 1-MATH-18 — Partition shapes into halves and fourths

**Mastery objective:** Partition circles and rectangles into two and four equal shares, describe the shares as halves/fourths or quarters, and explain that equal shares of the same whole must be the same size, with at least 85% accuracy across two separate demonstrations.

**Required evidence:** A draw-and-partition task plus an oral or written explanation distinguishing equal shares from unequal pieces.

**Prerequisites:** 1-MATH-17

**Primary lesson plan:** 5 lessons — 1 instruction, 1 guided practice, 1 independent practice, 1 application, 1 assessment.

### 1-MATH-19 — Add within 100 using place-value strategies

**Mastery objective:** Add within 100 using place-value reasoning and concrete or visual models, including a two-digit number plus a one-digit number and a two-digit number plus a multiple of 10, and explain how tens and ones change, with at least 85% accuracy across two separate demonstrations.

**Required evidence:** A mixed written addition-within-100 check plus a base-ten/modeling task requiring the student to explain tens-and-ones reasoning.

**Prerequisites:** 1-MATH-04, 1-MATH-06, 1-MATH-09

**Primary lesson plan:** 10 lessons — 2 instruction, 2 guided practice, 2 independent practice, 2 application, 2 assessment.

### 1-MATH-20 — Find 10 more and 10 less mentally

**Mastery objective:** Given a two-digit number, mentally determine the number that is 10 more or 10 less and explain that the tens digit changes while the ones digit remains the same, with at least 90% accuracy across two separate demonstrations.

**Required evidence:** Two short mental-math checks on different days plus an oral place-value explanation for selected items.

**Prerequisites:** 1-MATH-04, 1-MATH-05

**Primary lesson plan:** 5 lessons — 1 instruction, 1 guided practice, 1 independent practice, 1 application, 1 assessment.

### 1-MATH-21 — Subtract multiples of 10 using place-value reasoning

**Mastery objective:** Subtract a multiple of 10 from a multiple of 10 within the range 10–90 using place-value reasoning, models, or related facts and explain the tens-based strategy, with at least 85% accuracy across two separate demonstrations.

**Required evidence:** A written multiples-of-10 subtraction check plus a model or oral explanation connecting the problem to tens and related basic facts.

**Prerequisites:** 1-MATH-04, 1-MATH-07, 1-MATH-20

**Primary lesson plan:** 5 lessons — 1 instruction, 1 guided practice, 1 independent practice, 1 application, 1 assessment.
